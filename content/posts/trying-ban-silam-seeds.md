On a random summer day this year, I saw a poster at my neighborhood shop indicating they have it. It was written "यहाँ बन सिलाम पाइन्छ।" on a plain white paper with black marker - a conventional marketing by Nepalese shops. Initially I did not care much about it. However, later in few days, somebody told me that it has become much popular in Nepal.

<img alt="ban silam advert" height="576" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiv8QJWRjX21fp63rkjeAxPo4FbMU_tEX0EM7zT7JHbo8GtMq-bCmWQf9GYsrvVXTeZDFdqCKkxEiJtRs-HErBdcOlkVzQThG0wZXkCcNWxijcY7LySEbt4J09IlS3bzJBTGeUKjD1M0EGV-yAQ54c1jsQObxxlNUqaCim2p3LbuzqCE-jkVAPTSR5SL1o/s1600-rw/ban-silam-wild-basil-seeds.jpg" title="ban silam - himalayan basil advert" width="1280" />


<p>Hence, I decided to try it. I went to the shop and asked the shopkeeper about its benefits. He said that it is said to heal the body pain. The price was NRs 1000 per kg. I decided to buy 250 gm. He suggested that I needed to keep the seeds in an air-tight dry container.</p>

<img alt="ban silam seeds" border="0" data-original-height="576" data-original-width="1280" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhBKSWbvwS48WoaM2cWIGtU1KHfloDRm1Ujt1hasiKx85uJwGqLzPnaxNXDmmWwlk6wB3g6vrHsHR-DLPxiuKMOqbnoAFb6onwxIiXF076MjDXMCelFWuL29fEp3KwJl-ONHYR1nbXFxGq-xWIeIAjd0xmZTpHlR-zuTmlKsMo576YDZc4f8hChjxvrT0w/s1600-rw/ban-silam-wild-basil-seeds1.jpg" title="ban silam (himalayan basil) seeds" />

<p>At home, I washed half spoonful of seeds and kept it in drinking water overnight. In the morning, I saw that the seeds were surrounded by jelly-like balls. I knew that it would happen as I had researched a bit more about it. What next? - I consumed the water with few seeds. I  must admit that I could not drink the whole water with seeds because of its taste, or my body's response.</p>

<img alt="ban silam seeds" border="0" height="576" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgan-81A9uUA27hdMl0XHBkhDOGYzatubY9yhGyDJ9QhMwtUZ8VzuzmaszyMEErY4lwDQPRVTtnCBJi1pLwPOjF7IufwIPnpTJnRZk7s6YxmgwJpFZIZGhtv4Y8qyjkJEHVQzW4sO3gEEHaC-GtzJwsbgXkfz9DaX-7gILHua-33tGDxGjl4_xZ2ZmzDVw/s1600-rw/ban-silam-wild-basil-seeds2.jpg" title="ban silam (wild basil) seeds" width="1280" />

<p>What's next? Perhaps, I could try roasting the seeds and mixing with morning breakfast (muesli and milk).</p>
