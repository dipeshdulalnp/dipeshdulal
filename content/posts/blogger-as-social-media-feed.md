I have not completely left Google's Blogger paltform. I have been maintaining one blog with it. Let me see how it goes.

However, there is yet another reason for me to use Google's Blogger platform - its 'Reading List' section.

I follow few favourite blog websites. Surprisingly, this 'Reading List' section also brings updates from websites built on WordPress. I have not yet tried to get blogs from substack, medium, or other sources. The bloggers have been posting regularly which I would love to read. Now, let me affirm that reading long texts have been joyful experience to me after surviving Facebook deletion.

So, when I log in to Blogger platform and click 'reading list' from the vertical menu, it feeds me the snippets of the recent blog posts made in the blog-websites that I have followed by using Blogger platform.

To subscribe to your favourite blogs, just use [this link](https://draft.blogger.com/dashboard/reading/manage) if you have Blogger account and would like to use the same for getting blog updates from them. All you have to do is click 'Add'.

Happy reading!
