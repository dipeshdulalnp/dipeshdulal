Blogger as Social Media Feed

October 20, 2026 • 1 min read •
Categories:
  • Blogging
  • Reading

Name it content addiction - but I want to get recent views and updates from the people who write blogs that I like to read.

I have not completely left Google’s Blogger platform. I have been maintaining one blog with it. Let me see how it goes.

However, there is yet another reason for me to use Blogger - its ‘Reading List’ section.

I follow few favourite blog websites. Surprisingly, this ‘Reading List’ section also brings updates from websites built on WordPress. I have not yet tried to get blog updates from substack, medium, or other sources. My favourite bloggers have been posting regularly which I would love to read. Now, let me affirm that reading long texts have been joyful experience to me after surviving Facebook deletion .

So, when I log in to Blogger platform and click ‘Reading List’ from the vertical menu, it feeds me the snippets of the recent blog posts made in the blog-websites that I have followed.

To subscribe to your favourite blogs, just use this link if you have Blogger account and would like to use the same for getting blog updates from them. All you have to do is click ‘Add’.

Happy reading!
