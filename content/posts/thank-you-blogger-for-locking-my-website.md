Blogger locked my new website again! It was not new for me. It did time and again to the point that I heavily tempted to reconsider using it - why on Earth is this anti-spam measure hitting me?

As a professional gesture, they do notify both in Blogger dashboard and via email.

I became so frustrated that I was thinking of moving my entire set of websites to self-hosted WordPress, or run in Hugo.

I again contemplated - 
> Why do I lose in this anti-spam movement? It should be win-win for both Blogger and me! Blogger has done it as their anti-spam measure that they have put in place.

> In fact, I should work along with them.

Hence, I used to request them to review!

![Blogger Website Review](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEijFws-BLLnbmfT7-0J810mEsxI6kyPWYPdt93oSR2GZ_IhUZCXkf9_dp2u4MmCcBkORNbq8H-hrzmdDsLJm0jkYFkeWyJ6-eigdbl6EHdz0OmTANrjJzh4mKlKT133pYOhbLe4yomQpjRn2yrD1KvKh9LqeonEhZSOkesP1Nj3Bpb6wL9WG5p8_stT6Aw/s1600/reviving-blogger-blog.gif)

Voilà!

They always revived the website as my websites were not intended for spamming the internet space!

**UPDATE:**

I have moved 2 of my websites to other platforms whereas I still log on to Blogger for reading new posts from my reading list (mostly Blogger-based and WordPress-based blogs) and managing one blog.
